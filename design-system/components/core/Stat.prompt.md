Big serif figure with an uppercase label — for authority bands and metric grids.

```jsx
<Stat value="+50" label="Municipios" />
<Stat value="25" label="Años" accent />
<Stat value="+100" label="Proyectos" onDark align="center" />
```

`accent` makes the figure teal; `onDark` for ink sections; `align` left or center.
