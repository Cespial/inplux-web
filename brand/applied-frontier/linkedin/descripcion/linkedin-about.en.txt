There is a frontier between what AI can already do and what a public institution can actually use. On one side, capabilities that move every week. On the other, case files, regulations, deadlines and accountability. Almost no one crosses it.

INPLUX is an agentic software factory working exactly at that crossing. We turn legal, regulatory and institutional problems into software that reaches production: usable, verifiable, and with someone answerable for it.

"Agentic" describes how we work, not who decides. AI takes part in research, development, testing and documentation in a coordinated way; critical decisions and validation stay human. In legaltech and government that is not a nuance — it is the entry requirement.

Our work is in production across public procurement, tax, law and arbitration — Gobia, TribAI, Kelsen and Laudos.

Applied Frontier: frontier capability, applied all the way.
